Manual output layout for Atmos and decoded master material; excluded from automatic detection. It does not claim the source contains 20 discrete channels. Headphone/phone output remains binaural stereo.

Source: https://www.dolby.com/siteassets/about/support/guide/setup-guides/11.1.8-mounted-overhead-speakers-setup-guide/sell-sheet-11.1.8-mounted2.pdf

11 ear-level speakers: center, front ±30°, wide ±60°, surround ±100°, surround 1 ±122°, rear ±140°. 8 elevated speakers: front height ±30°/+25°, top front ±45°/+45°, top rear ±135°/+45°, rear height ±150°/+25°. One LFE.

These are SDA nominal directions within the guide ranges, not exact coordinates mandated by Dolby. The guide's side-view angles are projected angles: the chosen top directions project to approximately 54.7° elevation and the height directions to approximately 28.3°, within the illustrated ranges.

The built-in studio is a 6 × 5 × 3.2 m rectangular image-source simulation with speakers at 70% of the distance to the wall boundary (25 cm wall clearance before scaling), preserving the nominal azimuth/elevation. Speaker distances therefore vary. It uses actual recomputed responses for all 19 directional speakers. It is not a measured or Dolby-certified room. ITU 22.2 remains a separate layout and is not relabeled as Dolby.
